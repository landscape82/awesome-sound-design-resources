- **[Cakewalk Sonar](https://www.cakewalk.com/sonar)** 🆓 🖥️ - DAW for Windows (successor to Cakewalk by BandLab) with multi-track recording, editing, and mixing; the free tier covers the essentials, advanced features require a BandLab Membership.
- **[AudioMoth](https://www.openacousticdevices.info/audiomoth)** 💰 - Low-cost, open-source acoustic logger for long-term unattended recording of wildlife and environmental sound.
- **[BirdNET](https://birdnet.cornell.edu/)** 🆓 📱 🌐 - Machine-learning tool for identifying bird species in field recordings.
- **[BWF MetaEdit](https://mediaarea.net/BWFMetaEdit)** 🆓 🖥️ - Open-source editor for embedding, validating and exporting Broadcast WAV metadata in recordings.
- **[radio aporee ::: maps](https://aporee.org/maps/)** 🆓 🌐 - Global sound map of field recordings, open for listening and contributing.
- **[VolcaShare](https://volcashare.com/)** 🆓 🌐 - Online patch-sharing tool with browser emulators of the KORG Volca Bass and Volca Keys.
- **[BBC Sound Effects](https://sound-effects.bbcrewind.co.uk/)** 💰 🌐 - Archive of over 30,000 BBC sound effects, free for personal, educational and research use.
- **[MoSQITo](https://github.com/Eomys/MoSQITo)** 🆓 - Python toolbox of sound quality metrics, including loudness, sharpness, roughness and tonality.
